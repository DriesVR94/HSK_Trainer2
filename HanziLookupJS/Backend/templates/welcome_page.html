About HSK

The HSK Exam (Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì, 汉语水平考试) is the official Chinese language proficiency test used globally to assess non-native speakers' proficiency in Chinese. Originally comprising six different levels and 5,000 words, the new HSK version 3.0 now consists of nine levels and 11,000 words.

About the Creator

Hi, I'm Dries aka 云飞，

In 2012, I made one of the best decisions of my life and started learning Chinese. After four years of studying, I was super proud to pass the HSK Level 6 exam, which eventually helped me land a job in Beijing.

In 2026, a new version of HSK was announced—with three additional levels and around 6,000 more words to learn. By now, life has gotten a lot busier than when I was studying, so I needed a more efficient way to master all those new words.

That’s when I got the itch to build my own HSK writing practice tool. And that’s exactly how HSK Hero came to life.
